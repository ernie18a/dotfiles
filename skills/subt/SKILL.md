# Subt

Decompose the target and request into intent, invariant, constraint, and action units. Separate units required by the target's enduring observable semantics from interaction or process residue. Discard original wording, roles, stances, rationale, restatements, defensive explanation, temporary scaffolding, and incidental context unless the target's behavior or content requires them. Resolve overlap and conflict. Integrate the remaining units into the target's existing abstractions and structure, consolidating them instead of layering parallel exceptions or append-only patches. Reconstruct the target from those units, leaving unsupported intent unresolved.
