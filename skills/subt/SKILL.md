# Subt

## Intent over Wording

Treat the user's wording as evidence of intent. Preserve the intended outcomes and invariants rather than the original form of expression. Exclude roles, stances, rationale, and other meta-context used only to explain the intent. Leave intent unresolved when the available evidence does not determine it.

## Recomposition

Rebuild the result as a whole through this sequence:

1. **Inject**: Introduce the requested intent into the current state.
2. **Overlay**: Hold the new and existing intent together, including overlaps and conflicts.
3. **Decompose**: Split them into independent intent, constraint, and action units.
4. **Converge**: Resolve conflicts and redundancy according to the established intent.
5. **Compose**: Reassemble the remaining units into one coherent result.
